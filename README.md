# Canada Acquisition

**Buy a small business in Canada, step by step, with Claude or ChatGPT.**

A free toolkit of 27 skills covering every stage of buying a small business in Canada: deciding what to buy, finding and screening deals, valuing a business, financing (BDC, CSBFP, seller financing and targeted programs), writing an offer, closing, and your first 90 days as owner.

Before it gives advice, it asks a few questions about you and the deal: your province, budget, experience and the business you're looking at. That way the answers fit your situation instead of being generic.

## Install

| | **Option 1: Download** | **Option 2: Install with updates** |

### Claude (desktop app or claude.ai)

**Option 1: Download**
1. Download [`canada-acquisition.plugin`](plugin/canada-acquisition.plugin).
2. In Claude, open **Customize → Plugins**.
3. Choose **Upload** and select the file.

**Option 2: Install with updates**
1. In Claude, open **Customize → Plugins**.
2. Choose **Add marketplace** and paste: `mamandahub/canada-acquisition`
3. Find **canada-acquisition** in the list and click **Install**.

## What's inside

### Workflows (start here)

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `acquisition-workflow-map` | Works out which stage you're at and points you to the right workflow |
| `acquisition-roadmap` | Buy box, best-fit sectors, and a 30-day plan for finding deals |
| `seller-call-prep` | Seller type, top questions, and red flags before a call |
| `deal-screen` | Red, yellow, or green decision on a listing or teaser |
| `offer-structure` | Valuation range, financing structure, and LOI terms |
| `loi-review` | Reviews a draft LOI for missing Canadian protections and weak terms |
| `profile-aware-financing` | Financing options matched to you and the deal |
| `lender-package-prep` | Lender-ready package, DSCR story, and document checklist |
| `close-and-grow` | Path from LOI to funding, then a 90-day plan |
| `platform-or-cfe` | Consulting-for-equity or roll-up when a deal isn't ready to buy |

## Important

This toolkit is for education and planning. It is **not legal, tax, or financial advice**. Market multiples, interest rates, and program details reflect the time they were written. Confirm current terms with your lender, lawyer, and accountant before you rely on them.
