# Canada Acquisition

A toolkit for buying a small business in Canada, from choosing what to buy to running the business in its first 90 days. It is Canada-specific throughout: BDC and CSBFP financing, seller VTBs, targeted programs (BELF, Futurpreneur, regional agencies), CRA clearance, and Section 116 and Section 167 elections.

## How it works

Every skill asks a few clarifying questions before it researches or drafts anything. It asks about your province, budget, role, experience, and the deal in front of you, so the answer fits your situation instead of being generic. It asks everything missing in one message, won't ask again for something you've already said, and lists its assumptions if you tell it to go ahead without answering. Identity questions used to match financing programs are always optional.

## Workflows (start here)

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `acquisition-workflow-map` | Figures out which stage you're at and routes to the right workflow |
| `acquisition-roadmap` | Buy box, best-fit sectors, and a 30-day sourcing plan |
| `seller-call-prep` | Seller archetype, top questions, and red flags before a call |
| `deal-screen` | Red, yellow, or green decision on a listing or teaser |
| `offer-structure` | Valuation range, financing structure, and LOI terms |
| `loi-review` | Reviews a draft LOI for missing Canadian protections and weak terms |
| `profile-aware-financing` | Capital stack matched to your buyer profile and deal |
| `lender-package-prep` | Lender-ready package, DSCR story, and document checklist |
| `close-and-grow` | Closing path from LOI to funding, then a 90-day plan |
| `platform-or-cfe` | Consulting-for-equity or roll-up when a deal isn't buy-now ready |

## Specialist skills

`defining-buy-box`, `market-intelligence`, `sourcing-deals`, `analyzing-seller-psychology`, `vetting-deals`, `transfer-of-value-analysis`, `red-light-green-light-analysis`, `valuing-businesses`, `structuring-deals`, `sourcing-capital`, `making-offers`, `building-deal-teams`, `financial-due-diligence`, `closing-deals`, `post-acquisition-planning`, `consulting-for-equity`, `roll-up-acquisitions`

Each has a `reference.md` with tables, checklists, and templates.

## Recommended order

Buy box → market → sourcing → seller read → vet → transfer of value → go/no-go → value → structure → capital → offer → team → diligence → close → post-close. Branch to consulting-for-equity or roll-up when the target calls for it.

## Notes

Market multiples, rates, and program details in the reference files reflect the time they were written. Confirm current terms with lenders and advisors before relying on them. This is not legal, tax, or financial advice.
