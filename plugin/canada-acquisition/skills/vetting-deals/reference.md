# Vetting Deals — Canadian Reference

## Full Vetting Checklist (5 sections × 5 pts)

### Section 1: Business Fundamentals

- [ ] Operating 10+ years
- [ ] Revenue consistent or growing (not declining 3+ years)
- [ ] EBITDA positive and sustainable
- [ ] Not entirely owner-dependent (has staff, systems, processes)
- [ ] No single customer >25–30% of revenue

### Section 2: Canadian Market Fit

- [ ] Located in province with active deal financing (ON, BC, AB, QC preferred)
- [ ] Qualifies for BDC or chartered bank lending (3+ years operating, profitable)
- [ ] Revenue $500K–$10M / EBITDA $200K–$2M
- [ ] Industry is BDC-eligible (not cannabis, not speculative)

### Section 3: Seller Profile

- [ ] Clear, believable reason for selling
- [ ] Seller appears motivated (not testing the market)
- [ ] No obvious legal issues, liens, or judgments in public records

### Section 4: Deal Structure Viability

- [ ] Asking price in reasonable multiple range (2x–5x EBITDA for Canada)
- [ ] Deal size is financeable (BDC, bank, or seller finance viable)
- [ ] Seller shows some flexibility on structure

### Section 5: Reputation

- [ ] Google reviews ≥ 4.0 stars, no unresolved complaints
- [ ] BBB Canada — no serious complaints (bbb.org/canada)
- [ ] Corporations Canada shows active incorporation
- [ ] Owner LinkedIn / news search returns nothing alarming

## Industry Quick Reference

**High value (BDC-friendly):** HVAC, plumbing, electrical, commercial cleaning, insurance brokerages, accounting firms, IT MSP, niche manufacturing, home care

**Proceed with caution:** Restaurants (hard to finance), retail (margin pressure), trucking (asset-heavy, thin margins), construction (project-based, cyclical)

**Generally avoid:** Startups <3 years, single-owner no-staff businesses, structurally declining industries

## First Contact Questions

1. How long has this been listed?
2. Why is the owner selling?
3. How involved is the owner daily?
4. Revenue trend over 3 years?
5. Any CRA issues, outstanding taxes, or liens?
6. Management team in place?
7. Lease situation — term remaining and renewal?
8. Any prior LOI or deal that fell through? Why?
9. Is the seller open to any seller financing or deferred consideration?

## Hard Pass Triggers

- Seller refuses to provide basic financials after initial interest is established
- Revenue cannot be reconciled to tax filings or bank statements
- Seller is not authorized to sell or ownership is disputed
- Business survives only because owner underpays themselves and has no replacement path
- Licence, lease, or core contract cannot be assigned or replaced

## Fast Yellow Triggers

- High owner dependence but clear transition willingness
- Books are messy but rebuildable
- Short lease runway but renewal discussions are active
- Asking price high, but seller is flexible on terms

## Recommended Screen Output Format

1. Deal snapshot
2. Score by section
3. Top 3 strengths
4. Top 3 risks
5. RAG outcome
6. Clarifications required before LOI
