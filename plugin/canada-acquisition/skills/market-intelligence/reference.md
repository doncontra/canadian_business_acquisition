## Financing Rate Context

All Canadian acquisition loan rates float with the Bank of Canada rate. Before advising on specific rates:

1. Check current Bank of Canada overnight rate at bankofcanada.ca
2. BDC prime = Bank of Canada rate + ~2.45%
3. Bank prime = Bank of Canada rate + ~2.20%
4. BDC acquisition loans: prime + 1.5% to 3.5% (risk-based)
5. Chartered bank acquisition loans: prime + 1% to 3% (with collateral)

## How to Answer a Market Question

When producing market intelligence, always cover:

1. Sector multiple range by EBITDA size
2. Financing appetite for that sector in Canada
3. Province-specific buyer competition
4. Tailwinds and headwinds over the next 12 to 24 months
5. Whether this is a good first-acquisition sector or better for experienced buyers

## Common Sector Warnings

- Restaurants: operationally intensive, margin-sensitive, hard to finance
- Retail: weaker multiple support unless niche and recurring
- Construction: good sellers exist, but backlog quality and working capital swings matter
- Professional practices: attractive, but licensing, retention, and structure must be handled carefully
