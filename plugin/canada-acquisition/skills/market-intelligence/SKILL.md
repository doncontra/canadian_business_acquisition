# Market Intelligence

Provides Canada-specific acquisition market context. See [reference.md](reference.md) for multiple tables, sector analysis, regional notes, and key data sources.

## Before you start: ask, then tailor

Good answers depend on who the buyer is and what the deal looks like. Don't research or draft until you have the facts below.

1. Use what the user has already said in this conversation, including answers given to another skill. Never ask for it again.
2. Ask for what's missing in one message: no more than 6 questions, numbered, most important first. Give example answers so each is quick to reply to. Use an ask-question tool if one is available.
3. Only ask what would change the answer. Skip anything with a sensible default.
4. If the user has already given everything, skip the questions and start.
5. If the user says to go ahead without answering, proceed and list your assumptions at the top of the output.
6. Start the output with a one-line recap of the facts you're working from, so the user can correct them.

Ask about:

- Sector, and province or city
- What it's for: choosing a sector, pricing a specific deal, or timing
- Deal size range

## Workflow

- [ ] Identify the sector and province the user is asking about
- [ ] Provide current multiple range for that sector/size
- [ ] Assess financing conditions (BDC active? Bank appetite?)
- [ ] Identify sector tailwinds and headwinds
- [ ] Give regional context (Ontario vs. BC vs. Alberta vs. Quebec nuances)
- [ ] Recommend whether now is a good time to buy in this space
- [ ] Point to verification sources for current data

## Canadian Market Snapshot

- ~87,000–90,000 businesses for sale in Canada at any given time
- Baby boomer retirement wave accelerating deal supply through 2035
- Average lower mid-market multiple: 3.0x–3.5x EBITDA (trending down — more sellers than buyers)
- BDC actively lending; chartered banks requiring 20–30% equity
- Deal flow concentrated in Ontario, BC, Alberta; Atlantic Canada has least buyer competition

## Important: verify current rates

Interest rates change with Bank of Canada decisions. Always check:
- Bank of Canada overnight rate: bankofcanada.ca
- BDC prime rate: bdc.ca (affects all acquisition loan pricing)

Do not quote specific interest rates without confirming current Bank of Canada rate first.

## Output

Current market conditions (buyer's or seller's market in this segment), multiple benchmarks, financing availability, sector trends, regional factors, timing recommendation, data sources to verify.
