# Acquisition Workflow Map

Identify where the user is in the Canadian acquisition process, then load the matching workflow or skills. Ask one question only if the stage is genuinely unclear. Don't collect buyer or deal details here: the workflow you route to asks its own tailored questions.

## Stage Map

| Stage | Goal | Workflow skill | Core skills | Typical output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Strategy | Decide what to buy and where | `acquisition-roadmap` | `defining-buy-box`, `market-intelligence` | Buy box, target sectors, province focus |
| 2. Pipeline | Build deal flow | `acquisition-roadmap` | `sourcing-deals` | 30-day sourcing plan, referral targets |
| 3. Seller read | Understand motivation and fit | `seller-call-prep` | `analyzing-seller-psychology` | Seller archetype, motivation score, structure fit |
| 4. Quick screen | Decide whether to spend more time | `deal-screen` | `vetting-deals`, `transfer-of-value-analysis`, `red-light-green-light-analysis` | Red, yellow, or green decision |
| 5. Value | Estimate fair price | `offer-structure` | `valuing-businesses`, `transfer-of-value-analysis` | Floor, target, ceiling valuation |
| 6. Structure | Design financing and terms | `offer-structure`, `profile-aware-financing` | `structuring-deals`, `sourcing-capital`, `analyzing-seller-psychology` | Capital stack, DSCR, structure options |
| 7. Offer | Draft and present LOI | `offer-structure`, `loi-review` | `making-offers` | LOI summary, seller talking points |
| 8. Financing | Approach lenders | `lender-package-prep` | `sourcing-capital`, `financial-due-diligence` | Lender package, document checklist |
| 9. Team setup | Fill advisor and partner gaps | `close-and-grow` | `building-deal-teams` | Team gap list and recruiting plan |
| 10. Diligence | Confirm earnings and risks | `close-and-grow` | `financial-due-diligence`, `closing-deals` | Risk summary, closing checklist |
| 11. Close | Move from LOI to funded close | `close-and-grow` | `closing-deals` | Critical path plan and close steps |
| 12. Stabilize | Protect value after closing | `close-and-grow` | `post-acquisition-planning` | 90-day action plan |
| 13. Alternative path | Staged entry when not buy-now ready | `platform-or-cfe` | `consulting-for-equity`, `transfer-of-value-analysis` | CFE structure or milestone plan |
| 14. Scale | Build a platform and bolt-ons | `platform-or-cfe` | `roll-up-acquisitions` | Platform thesis and bolt-on roadmap |

## Common Starting Points

- "I don't know what business to buy" → `acquisition-roadmap`
- "I found a listing; is it any good?" → `deal-screen`
- "I have a call with the owner" → `seller-call-prep`
- "Seller wants too much; how do I structure it?" → `offer-structure`
- "Can you check my LOI?" → `loi-review`
- "I need to go to BDC / the bank" → `lender-package-prep`
- "I have an LOI signed" → `close-and-grow`
- "Good company but not financeable yet" → `platform-or-cfe`

## Output

State the stage in one line, name the workflow being run, then run it.
