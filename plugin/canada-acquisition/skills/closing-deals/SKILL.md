# Closing Deals

Manages every phase from accepted LOI to funded close. See [reference.md](reference.md) for the full closing checklist, common obstacles and solutions, and first 72-hour post-close actions.

## Before you start: ask, then tailor

Good answers depend on who the buyer is and what the deal looks like. Don't research or draft until you have the facts below.

1. Use what the user has already said in this conversation, including answers given to another skill. Never ask for it again.
2. Ask for what's missing in one message: no more than 6 questions, numbered, most important first. Give example answers so each is quick to reply to. Use an ask-question tool if one is available.
3. Only ask what would change the answer. Skip anything with a sensible default.
4. If the user has already given everything, skip the questions and start.
5. If the user says to go ahead without answering, proceed and list your assumptions at the top of the output.
6. Start the output with a one-line recap of the facts you're working from, so the user can correct them.

Ask about:

- LOI date and key deadlines
- Asset or share purchase, and province
- Financing status
- Who's on your team already
- Items still outstanding from the seller

## Closing Timeline

```
Week 1–2:   LOI signed → assemble deal team, request due diligence docs, apply for CRA clearance
Week 2–6:   Due diligence (financial, legal, operational)
Week 4–8:   Financing application (BDC / bank)
Week 6–10:  Purchase Agreement drafting and negotiation
Week 8–14:  Satisfy lender conditions (site visit, appraisals, management presentation)
Week 10–16: Lease assignment, employment consents, remaining conditions
Week 12–18: Close and fund
```

**BDC/bank deals: 90–120 days typical.**
**Seller-financed / annuity deals: 2–4 weeks possible.**

## Workflow

- [ ] **Phase 1 — Setup**: Assemble deal team, open deal folder, request all documents, confirm exclusivity dates
- [ ] **Phase 2 — Due diligence**: Financial, legal, and operational review complete
- [ ] **Phase 3 — Financing**: Lender application submitted, conditions satisfied
- [ ] **Phase 4 — Purchase Agreement**: Negotiated and signed by all parties
- [ ] **Phase 5 — Conditions**: CRA clearance, lease assignment, employee retention, regulatory licences
- [ ] **Phase 6 — Close**: Funds wired, documents signed, access transferred, employees notified

## Critical path items (delays kill deals)

- **CRA clearance certificate** — apply in week 1; takes 4–6 weeks
- **Lender information requests** — respond same day; never let these sit
- **Lease assignment** — start landlord conversation early; some take weeks
- **Key employee retention** — identify and secure before close is announced

## Output

Current phase assessment, outstanding items list, critical path risks, next 7-day action plan.
