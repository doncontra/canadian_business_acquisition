# Sourcing Deals — Canadian Reference

## On-Market Sources

Scan in this order: national sites, then the province's local brokers, then niche sites for the target industry. Local brokers often post listings days before they reach the national sites.

### National

| Site | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| BusinessesForSale.com Canada (canada.businessesforsale.com) | Largest Canadian volume. Search by province and city, then category. Filter out "Sold STC." |
| BizBuySell (bizbuysell.com, province pages such as /alberta-canada/) | Fewer Canadian listings, some not on BusinessesForSale. |
| realtor.ca (commercial, property type "Business") | MLS business listings from realtors. Many are restaurants and franchises, and many are leases, so filter for "For Sale." Zolo.ca and provincial MLS mirrors show the same feed. |
| DealStream (dealstream.com, Canada and province pages) | Mostly US. Use the Canada and province pages only. |
| Business Sell Canada (businesssellcanada.com) | For-sale-by-owner classifieds, with province pages. Figures are often incomplete. |
| GlobalBX (globalbx.com, province pages) | Low-cost listings, many from owners. |
| Kijiji (Business for Sale category, by city) | A fallback for deals under $500K. Expect scams and missing figures. |
| Sunbelt Canada (sunbeltcanada.com, regional offices) | Each office posts its own listings. |
| Transworld Business Advisors Canada (tworld.ca) | Franchise broker network with Canadian offices. |
| MergersCorp Canada, Transact Capital Partners | Larger deals, usually $1M+ in EBITDA. |
| CBBA member broker listings (canadianbusinessbrokers.ca) | Directory of member brokers. |

### Public procurement (not business listings)

Alberta Purchasing Connection (purchasing.alberta.ca) and MERX (merx.com) list government contracts open for bidding, not businesses for sale. Use them to:

- Check a seller's claim that revenue comes from public contracts (look up past awards and who won them)
- Gauge demand in bid-driven categories such as snow removal, janitorial, waste, and well abandonment before buying a business in one
- Find the local companies that keep winning bids, as off-market outreach targets

## Off-Market Referral Network

| Contact Type                             | Why They Help                                 | Pitch                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| CPA / Chartered Accountant               | First to know clients are thinking of selling | "Do any clients want a confidential exit in the next 6–24 months?" |
| M&A / Estate Planning Lawyer             | Handles ownership transitions                 | "I'm a serious buyer — can we keep in touch as deals arise?"       |
| Wealth Manager / Financial Planner       | Baby boomer clients planning retirement       | "I provide a liquidity event for business-owner clients"           |
| Commercial Banker (RBC, TD, Scotia, BMO) | Sees business financials                      | "I'm pre-qualified — refer me to clients thinking of selling"      |
| BDC Advisor                              | Knows which businesses need succession help   | "I'm an acquisition entrepreneur actively closing deals"           |
| Exit Planning Advisors (CEPA)            | Specialize in seller readiness                | Natural referral partner                                           |

## Direct Outreach Scripts

**To CPAs:**

> "Hi [Name], I'm an acquisition entrepreneur based in [city]. I'm looking for retiring business owners who need a smooth, confidential exit. Do any of your clients fit that profile in the next 6–24 months?"

**To Business Owners:**

> "Hi [Name], I'm impressed by what you've built. I'm looking to acquire an established [industry] business in [region]. Would you be open to a confidential conversation about the future of your business?"

## Province-Specific Notes

- **Ontario / GTA**: Highest volume. Also look to Hamilton, London, Kitchener, Ottawa for better multiples.
- **BC / Vancouver**: Active in tech, construction. Try Kelowna/Kamloops for less competition.
- **Alberta**: ATB Financial very entrepreneur-friendly. Energy services + construction strong.
- **Quebec**: French language required for customer-facing businesses. Use Desjardins. Relationship-first culture.
- **Atlantic Canada**: Low volume, very low buyer competition. ACOA provides federal financing.

## Weekly Cadence

- Monday: Review new on-market listings
- Tue–Wed: 10–15 new off-market outreach contacts
- Thursday: Follow up with existing referral sources
- Friday: Log pipeline, update deal tracker

## 30-Day Pipeline Targets

- 20 to 40 broker or listing reviews
- 30 to 60 new off-market referral contacts
- 10 to 20 direct owner outreach attempts if buy box is narrow
- 5 to 10 live conversations with brokers or referral partners
- 2 to 5 seller conversations booked

## Deal Tracker Fields

- Company name
- Province and city
- Industry
- Revenue and EBITDA estimate
- Source channel
- Seller age or stated exit reason
- Last contact date
- Next step
- Initial RAG view

## Channel Selection Rules

- Narrow niche or rural market: lean harder into direct outreach and local intermediaries
- Competitive urban markets: rely more on referrals and speed of follow-up
- First-time buyer: focus on fewer industries, more repetition, and referral relationships over cold volume
