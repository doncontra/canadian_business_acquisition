# Canada Seller Call Prep

Prepare the user for an upcoming seller conversation on a Canadian acquisition target.

## Before you start: ask, then tailor

Good answers depend on who the buyer is and what the deal looks like. Don't research or draft until you have the facts below.

1. Use what the user has already said in this conversation, including answers given to another skill. Never ask for it again.
2. Ask for what's missing in one message: no more than 6 questions, numbered, most important first. Give example answers so each is quick to reply to. Use an ask-question tool if one is available.
3. Only ask what would change the answer. Skip anything with a sensible default.
4. If the user has already given everything, skip the questions and start.
5. If the user says to go ahead without answering, proceed and list your assumptions at the top of the output.
6. Start the output with a one-line recap of the facts you're working from, so the user can correct them.

This workflow loads several skills. Ask one combined set of questions for the whole workflow; the loaded skills should not ask again.

Ask about:

- The listing, broker notes, or anything written about the business (paste or link)
- What you know about the owner: age, years owned, stated reason for selling
- How the call came about (broker or off-market), who will be on it, and whether it's a first call or a follow-up
- Financials you've seen so far
- What you most want out of the call: price flexibility, seller financing, transition, or just rapport

## Load these skills first

- `analyzing-seller-psychology`
- `vetting-deals`
- `transfer-of-value-analysis`

## Workflow

1. Ask for any available context: listing, broker notes, industry, province, owner background, and known financials.
2. Infer the most likely seller archetype and probable motivation level.
3. Build a prioritized seller call agenda.
4. Produce the best discovery questions to test motivation, owner dependence, transferability, and structure flexibility.
5. Flag the top red flags to watch for during the call.
6. Suggest the best likely structure angles to probe, such as seller financing, deferred consideration, or transition support.

## Output

Provide:

- seller archetype hypothesis
- likely motivation level
- top 10 seller-call questions
- top 5 red flags to test
- likely structure angles to explore
- recommended next step after the call
