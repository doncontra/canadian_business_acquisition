# Making Offers — Canadian Reference

## LOI Template

```
LETTER OF INTENT

Date: [Date]
From: [Buyer Name / Entity]
To: [Seller Name]
Re: Proposed Acquisition of [Business Name]

Dear [Name],

This Letter of Intent outlines proposed terms for [Buyer] ("Buyer") to acquire
[Business Name] ("Business") from [Seller] ("Seller").

1. PURCHASE PRICE
Total consideration: CAD $[X], payable as:
  - Cash at closing:  CAD $[X]
  - Seller VTB Note:  CAD $[X] at [X]% per annum over [X] years
  - Earnout:          Up to CAD $[X] based on [metric] for [period]

2. STRUCTURE
[Share Purchase / Asset Purchase] of [Business Name]
[If asset sale: Parties will jointly elect under Section 167 of the Excise Tax Act]

3. CONDITIONS
(a) Due diligence satisfactory to Buyer (30 days)
(b) Financing arranged on satisfactory terms
(c) Formal Purchase Agreement executed
(d) Landlord consent to lease assignment
(e) CRA account confirmed in good standing (no outstanding liens/remittances)
(f) No material adverse change to the Business before closing

4. EXCLUSIVITY
Seller grants Buyer exclusive negotiating rights for [45–60] days from acceptance.
Seller will not solicit or entertain other offers during this period.

5. TRANSITION
Seller will remain available for [60–90] days post-close for operational transition
at a mutually agreed compensation of CAD $[X]/month.

6. NON-COMPETE
Seller agrees to a [3–5]-year non-compete within [province/region/industry].

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
All terms and discussions strictly confidential.

8. TARGET CLOSING DATE
[Date — typically 60–90 days from LOI acceptance]

This LOI is non-binding except Clauses 4 (Exclusivity) and 7 (Confidentiality).

[Buyer Signature / Date]

Accepted: _________________ Date: _________
```

## Canadian-Specific Clauses

- **CRA Clearance Certificate**: Condition closing on seller obtaining CRA clearance confirming no outstanding tax obligations. Apply early — takes 4–6 weeks.
- **Section 116 (Non-Resident)**: If seller is non-resident, buyer must withhold 25% and remit to CRA — or obtain clearance certificate first. Include in conditions.
- **Section 167 Election** (asset sale): Jointly elect to treat sale as going concern → no HST payable on qualifying assets.
- **Working Capital Peg**: Include a target working capital amount at close to prevent seller stripping cash before closing.
- **R&W Holdback**: Typically 10–15% of price held in trust for 12–18 months to cover rep & warranty claims.
- **Quebec**: If the business is in Quebec, LOI may require French version. Engage a Quebec lawyer.

## Verbal Cover Script (Retiring Boomer Seller)

> "[Name], I want to make you an offer that actually works for you. Here's what I'm thinking.
> I'd like to buy the business at [price]. But the number that matters most isn't just the
> purchase price — it's what you actually walk away with after tax. By structuring this as a
> [share sale / deferred payment], you only pay capital gains tax as you receive each payment.
> Over the full term, you'd net significantly more than you would on a lump-sum all-cash deal.
> And I'm committed to this business for the long run — your team will be looked after."

## Negotiation Guidance

**Hold firm on:** Exclusivity period (protect your time), due diligence access, CRA clearance condition, non-compete.

**Negotiate on:** Price (within valuation range), payment timeline, VTB interest rate, transition period length and pay.

**Trade-off levers:** Offer share sale structure in exchange for lower price — seller often nets more after LCGE exemption.

## Two-Offer Approach

When helpful, show two options:

- Offer A: higher certainty, more lender-backed, lower headline price
- Offer B: higher headline price, more seller paper or deferred consideration

This lets the seller choose between certainty and total proceeds rather than arguing only about price.

## Concession Ladder

Concede in this order where needed:

1. Payment timing
2. Transition support terms
3. Earnout mechanics
4. Small price movement

Concede last on:

- Exclusivity
- DD access
- CRA and legal protection clauses
- Working capital protection
