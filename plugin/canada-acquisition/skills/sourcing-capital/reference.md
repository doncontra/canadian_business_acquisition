# Sourcing Capital — Canadian Reference

**Last checked: 28 September 2026.** Amounts and eligibility change often. Confirm with the lender before relying on a number.

## The basics

- The Canadian acquisition stack is led by **BDC**, **banks and credit unions**, **seller VTBs**, and **CSBFP** for asset purchases.
- Most grants and support programs do **not** fund goodwill or the seller's purchase price.
- Profile-based loan funds are usually $25K–$250K. Use them as companion capital to close an equity gap, not as the main lender.

## Program Matrix

"Who" is the profile a buyer needs to qualify. "Buying a business?" says whether the program can fund the purchase of an existing business.

### Newcomers, and other groups

No major Canadian loan fund is built for newcomers buying a business. Newcomers who are permanent residents qualify for the general programs above. Some banks run newcomer business banking packages, but those are accounts, not acquisition loans. For persons with disabilities, 2SLGBTQI+ entrepreneurs, and veterans, check provincial programs and Community Futures (some offices run disability-focused loans) rather than assuming a national fund exists.

## BDC (Business Development Bank of Canada)

- Website: bdc.ca
- Crown corporation mandated to support Canadian entrepreneurs
- Acquisition financing: $100K+, long amortization
- Cash flow lending, with no hard-asset requirement for service businesses
- Finances goodwill and share purchases, unlike CSBFP
- Very suitable for first-time acquirers with industry expertise
- Co-lends with Futurpreneur for buyers aged 18–39, and underwrites BELF loans above $100K
- Find your local BDC Business Centre: bdc.ca/en/about/offices-bdc-business-centres

## Grants And Non-Dilutive Programs

- **Hiring and wage subsidies**: Can offset early payroll risk after close, especially when retaining or adding staff.
- **Training grants**: Useful if the first 12 months require upskilling team members or owner-replacement capability.
- **Productivity and digital adoption support**: Helpful for CRM, ERP, automation, e-commerce, cybersecurity, or process upgrades after acquisition.
- **Export support**: Relevant if the target has U.S. or international growth potential and needs working capital or market-development support.

Treat these as EBITDA-protection tools, not purchase-price financing.

## Borrower Package Checklist

**Personal:**

- [ ] Personal financial statement (net worth)
- [ ] Last 2–3 years personal T1 returns (CRA Notice of Assessment)
- [ ] Resume/bio with relevant industry experience
- [ ] Equifax / TransUnion Canada credit report

**Business (target company):**

- [ ] 3 years T2 corporate returns
- [ ] 3 years financial statements
- [ ] Last 12 months bank statements
- [ ] Asset and liability list
- [ ] Lease agreements
- [ ] Normalized EBITDA analysis with add-backs explained

**Deal:**

- [ ] Signed LOI or purchase agreement
- [ ] Proposed deal structure and use of proceeds
- [ ] Purchase price allocation across assets (required for CSBFP)
- [ ] DSCR analysis
- [ ] Business plan for 12–24 months post-close
- [ ] Seller transition plan

## Alternative Lenders

- **Roynat Capital** (Scotiabank subsidiary): equipment-heavy businesses
- **Farm Credit Canada (FCC)**: agribusiness, food processing, rural
- **Export Development Canada (EDC)**: businesses with export revenue
- **Clearco / Thinking Capital**: e-commerce and digital businesses
- US SBA loans can't be used to buy a Canadian business.

## Recommended First-Pass Order

1. BDC
2. Seller VTB conversation
3. Bank or credit union in the target's province
4. CSBFP, if it's an asset purchase with equipment, leaseholds, or real estate
5. Profile-based programs the buyer qualifies for (from the Program Matrix), starting with the ones that allow buying a business
6. Community Futures, if the business is rural
7. Regional agency review for indirect, post-close support

## Lender Outreach Sequence

1. Introduce yourself and your buy box before you have a live deal if possible
2. Ask what EBITDA size, industry, and structure they like best
3. When live deal appears, send a clean summary with normalized EBITDA and DSCR first
4. Follow immediately with indexed backup documents
5. Keep all lender follow-up same day when possible

## Equity Partner Pitch Components

- Why this business is attractive
- Why now
- How the structure protects downside
- Operator credibility and post-close plan
- Clear path to refinance, distributions, or exit
