## Canadian Industry Sweet Spots

| Industry                        | Why                                                      | BDC-Friendly      |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| HVAC / Mechanical               | Recession-resistant, recurring contracts, high cash flow | Yes               |
| Electrical Contracting          | Licensed trades, barriers to entry                       | Yes               |
| Plumbing                        | Essential, loyal customer base                           | Yes               |
| Commercial Cleaning             | Recurring contracts, low capex                           | Yes               |
| Landscaping / Snow Removal      | Contract-based, seasonal but scalable                    | Yes               |
| Accounting / Bookkeeping Firms  | Annuity-like revenue, highly financeable                 | Yes               |
| Insurance Brokerages            | Recurring commissions, regulated (barriers to entry)     | Specialty lenders |
| Home Care / Healthcare Staffing | Aging population demand, government contracts            | Yes               |
| IT Managed Services (MSP)       | Recurring MRR, high margins                              | Yes               |
| Niche Manufacturing             | BDC mandate, often undervalued                           | Yes (priority)    |
| Auto Services                   | Essential, recession-resistant                           | Yes               |

## Buy Box Red Flags (Exclude These)

- Business is entirely dependent on the owner (no staff, no systems)
- Single customer > 30% of revenue
- Revenue declining 3+ consecutive years with no thesis
- Highly licensed industries you cannot get licensed in
- Outside your geographic comfort zone (for owner-operators)
- Cannabis (limited bank financing in Canada)

## Owner-Operator vs. Owner-Investor Decision Rules

| Buyer Type     | Best Fit                                                                | Watch-outs                                                           |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner-Operator | First acquisition, local service business, strong personal industry fit | Buying too large, too far away, or too regulated                     |
| Owner-Investor | Existing operator available, stronger capital access, portfolio mindset | Weak GM bench, poor reporting, overestimating remote control ability |
| Hybrid         | Buyer leads transition first, then installs operator later              | Buyer staying stuck in day-to-day operations too long                |

## Capital Fit by Deal Size

- Under $500K EBITDA: often best for first deals, partner deals, or seller-finance-heavy structures
- $500K to $1M EBITDA: strongest sweet spot for BDC-backed first acquisitions
- Above $1M EBITDA: better systems and financeability, but requires stronger team and capital plan
