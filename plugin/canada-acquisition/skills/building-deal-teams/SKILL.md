# Building Deal Teams

Identifies gaps in the user's deal team and provides specific actions to fill them. See [reference.md](reference.md) for role descriptions, Canadian advisor resources, and partner deal structures.

## Before you start: ask, then tailor

Good answers depend on who the buyer is and what the deal looks like. Don't research or draft until you have the facts below.

1. Use what the user has already said in this conversation, including answers given to another skill. Never ask for it again.
2. Ask for what's missing in one message: no more than 6 questions, numbered, most important first. Give example answers so each is quick to reply to. Use an ask-question tool if one is available.
3. Only ask what would change the answer. Skip anything with a sensible default.
4. If the user has already given everything, skip the questions and start.
5. If the user says to go ahead without answering, proceed and list your assumptions at the top of the output.
6. Start the output with a one-line recap of the facts you're working from, so the user can correct them.

Ask about:

- Deal stage and size, and province
- Who you already have: lawyer, CPA, lender, broker, mentor
- Whether you need a partner for capital, operations, or both
- Budget for advisors

## Core Roles for Any Canadian Deal

| Role | When Needed | Cost (per deal) |
|---|---|---|
| M&A Lawyer | LOI stage — must have | $3K–$15K |
| CPA / Chartered Accountant | Due diligence + tax planning | $2K–$8K |
| BDC Advisor or Commercial Banker | Any financed deal | Loan fees only |
| Business Broker | If on-market deal | Paid by seller (buy side is free) |
| Operations Partner / GM | If owner-investor structure | Salary + equity (5–15%) |
| Equity / Capital Partner | If buyer lacks down payment | Equity stake (20–40%) |
| Insurance Broker | At closing | Annual premium |

## Workflow

- [ ] Confirm which roles the user already has filled
- [ ] Identify gaps for the current deal
- [ ] Recommend specific resources by province to fill gaps
- [ ] If a capital partner is needed: define equity split and role division
- [ ] If an operator is needed: define compensation structure

## Key Canadian advisor resources

- **M&A Lawyers**: Law Society referral service (by province), Lexpert (lexpert.ca), Chambers Canada
- **CPAs**: CPA Canada member directory (cpacanada.ca); MNP, BDO, Grant Thornton are strong for Canadian M&A
- **BDC**: Find your Business Centre at bdc.ca/en/about/offices-bdc-business-centres
- **Brokers**: CBBA member directory (canadianbusinessbrokers.ca), Sunbelt Canada, Transact Capital

## Partner deal framework

Two-thirds of deals in acquisition communities are partner deals. Define upfront:
- Who operates (CEO/GM role, day-to-day control)
- Who capitalizes (equity stake, preferred return, no day-to-day role)
- Decision thresholds (what requires both partners to agree)

## Output

Roles required, gap assessment, recruitment actions by province, partner deal structure if needed.
