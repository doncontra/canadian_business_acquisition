# Post-Acquisition Planning — Canadian Reference

## 90-Day Plan Overview

### Week 1–2: Stabilize

- Meet all employees in person; 1-on-1s with every key person
- Key message: "I'm here to learn. No major changes right away."
- Call top 5–10 customers personally: introduce yourself, confirm continuity
- Call top 3–5 suppliers: introduce yourself, confirm payment terms
- Review cash position and next payroll cycle immediately
- Get all digital access: banking, email, accounting software, CRM, payroll

**Canadian employment note**: Review provincial employment standards on day 1. You cannot reduce compensation or make material changes without notice (constructive dismissal risk). Ontario ESA, BC Employment Standards Act, AB Employment Standards Code, Quebec Act Respecting Labour Standards — all apply differently.

### Week 3–4: Assess

- Review last 90 days of bank statements and cash flow
- Meet with your CPA to set up proper Canadian accounting going forward
- Identify top 20% customers driving 80% of revenue
- Identify top-performing employees vs. underperformers
- Map key processes (even if just verbal from seller)
- Review all open contracts and project pipeline
- Inventory equipment condition

### Month 2–3: Optimize

- Implement weekly reporting (revenue, expenses, cash, AR, pipeline)
- Set up QuickBooks Canada, Sage, or FreshBooks with Canadian tax accounts
- Register for CRA My Business Account access online
- Review pricing vs. market — are you leaving money on the table?
- Begin documenting top 5 critical SOPs
- Identify first visible "quick win" for the team
- Have career growth conversations with top employees

## Employee Retention Strategy

- **Retention bonuses**: Cash bonus for key employees who stay 12 months post-close
- **Transparency**: Be honest about your plans. Uncertainty is the enemy.
- **Don't overpromise**: Only commit to what you will actually do.
- **Promote your #2**: If there's a clear operations leader, elevate them publicly.

**Do not:** Unilaterally change compensation, titles, or responsibilities without proper notice — constructive dismissal risk under all provincial employment standards. **Quebec**: French language requirements apply to all workplace communications.

## Canadian Tax Optimization (Post-Close)

| Action                                 | Benefit                                          |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Confirm CCPC / QSBC status             | Preserve LCGE eligibility for future share sale  |
| Set salary vs. dividend split          | Minimize combined personal + corporate tax       |
| Maximize RRSP contributions            | Defer personal tax using business income         |
| Set up holding company                 | Asset protection; flexibility for future deals   |
| Track capital dividends account (CDA)  | Pay tax-free capital dividends from future gains |
| Review HST/GST registration and filing | Ensure compliance from day 1                     |

## Exit Planning Framework (Start Day 1)

**Private equity sale (most lucrative):**

- Timeline: 3–5 years
- PE threshold in Canada: $2M+ EBITDA, recurring revenue, owner-independent management
- Exit multiple: 5x–8x vs. ~3x you paid = significant multiple arbitrage

**Roll-up strategy:**

- Buy 1–2 competitors or adjacent businesses in same region/sector
- Consolidate under one management team
- Combined EBITDA attracts higher multiple
- BDC and alternative lenders actively support proven acquirers buying additional businesses

**Management buyout (MBO):**

- Develop your GM into the next buyer over 3–7 years
- Allows tax-efficient staged exit with continued upside

## First 30 Days: Do Not Do This

- Do not change compensation casually
- Do not announce a grand transformation plan before understanding the business
- Do not slash costs blindly and damage customer delivery
- Do not ignore seller handover discipline because "things seem fine"

## Day 30 / 60 / 90 Review Questions

- Day 30: What did I misunderstand at close?
- Day 60: Which employees and customers are truly central to value?
- Day 90: What are the top 3 levers that will expand EBITDA and exit multiple?
