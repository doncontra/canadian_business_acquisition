# Consulting for Equity — Canadian Reference

## Best Use Cases

- Seller is tired but the company is not clean enough for a lender yet
- Business has clear upside but weak systems or owner dependence
- Buyer has operating skill and credibility but limited cash
- Seller wants help monetizing the business before fully exiting

## Common Structures

### Structure A: Advisory Equity

- Buyer receives 10% to 25% equity
- Buyer leads a defined value-creation project
- No day-one control, but clear board or reporting rights

### Structure B: Working Buyout

- Buyer joins operations full or part time
- Seller retains majority initially
- Buyer earns equity through milestones and gets a later purchase option

### Structure C: Option to Acquire

- Buyer receives small initial equity or fee waiver
- Buyer gets exclusive option to buy additional shares at a formula price later
- Best when value needs to be proven first

### Structure D: Cash Plus Sweat

- Small buyer cash component plus defined operational improvements
- Seller paper or staged purchase reduces day-one cash need

## Milestone Examples

- EBITDA improved from $400K to $550K
- Owner replaced in estimating, scheduling, or sales role
- CRM and KPI dashboard implemented
- Top 20 customers transitioned to the team
- Two new recurring contracts signed

## Legal Issues to Cover

- Equity vesting schedule
- Default and termination rules
- Buy-sell provisions and valuation formula
- Decision rights and bank signing authority
- Non-compete and non-solicit restrictions
- Confidentiality and intellectual property ownership

## Canada-Specific Cautions

- Regulated sectors may require licence-holder control rules to be respected
- Clarify GST/HST responsibility if buyer is effectively operating before a formal purchase
- Use a Canadian lawyer for shareholder agreement and option drafting
- If the seller is non-resident, review withholding and structuring implications early

## Seller Framing

- "I can help you increase value before exit rather than forcing a rushed sale."
- "You keep upside while reducing your day-to-day burden."
- "We can create a path to a larger and safer exit instead of hoping a buyer appears today."
